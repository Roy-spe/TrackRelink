# Third-party data and attribution

No open-source license has been selected for the original TrackRelink code. Public availability does not relicense any third-party material.

The overview figure contains limited, cropped observations from DanceTrack0010 and DanceTrack0081 with TrackRelink annotations. Images belong to their original rights holders. The figures are included to explain the research examples; they do not grant permission to reuse the underlying photographs. The replay files contain derived tracker outputs and numerical features, not the dataset's original video collection or ground-truth annotation files.

The [official DanceTrack agreement](https://github.com/DanceTrack/DanceTrack#agreement) distinguishes its annotation license (CC BY 4.0), dataset access for non-commercial research, and MIT-licensed code. It states that the Internet-sourced images/videos are not owned by the dataset institutions. Do not treat the code license as an image license. Obtain the dataset from its official distributors and follow its terms for any further use.

Dataset reference: Peize Sun, Jinkun Cao, Yi Jiang, Zehuan Yuan, Song Bai, Kris Kitani and Ping Luo, *DanceTrack: Multi-Object Tracking in Uniform Appearance and Diverse Motion*, CVPR 2022.

Host and evaluation software are separate dependencies, not redistributed here:

- [Deep OC-SORT](https://github.com/GerardMaggiolino/Deep-OC-SORT)
- [StrongSORT / AFLink](https://github.com/dyhBUPT/StrongSORT)
- [TrackEval](https://github.com/JonathonLuiten/TrackEval)

NumPy, SciPy and scikit-learn are installed separately under their own licenses. The rendered figures embed font subsets; no font installation files are distributed.
